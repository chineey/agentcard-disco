How it works

01

Validate

Checks your agent-card.json against the A2A schema: required fields, URLs, auth schemes, and capabilities.

$ pip install agentcard-disco
02

Score

Rates discoverability 0–100 offline across 4 heuristic dimensions, or add --deep for Tier 2 AI quality analysis.

$ agentcard-disco score ./card.json --deep
03

Optimize

Surfaces prioritized, field-level fixes so orchestrators and A2A registries reliably route tasks to your agent.

$ agentcard-disco suggest ./card.json

Scoring grades & dimensions

85–100%

Grade A

Registry-ready. Rich metadata, distinct skills, realistic examples, and clear I/O modes.

70–84%

Grade B

Valid and discoverable, with a few metadata or completeness gaps worth tightening.

50–69%

Grade C

Valid but thin. Generic descriptions or missing tags make selection harder.

0–49%

Grade D / F

Significant gaps in specificity, search alignment, or protocol readiness. Fix before publishing.
